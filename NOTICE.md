# Third-party material

- Quran text: Tanzil Project (https://tanzil.net), license CC BY 3.0, verbatim use only, attribution required.
  Obtained through risan/quran-json (commit 6523a9ad). A variant without vowel marks is a modification and is not redistributed here.
- Hadith text and numbers: AhmedBaset/hadith-json (commit 70b83d6d), data scraped from sunnah.com. No license file was found,
  so no raw hadith text is redistributed in this package; only collection names and numbers appear as references.
- Models: inception42/Jais-3-32B, inception42/Jais-3-MoVA-36B-A4B, Qwen/Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct, Qwen/Qwen3-30B-A3B (see their model cards for licenses); GPT-4o via the OpenAI API.
- The jurisprudence background is taken from secondary sources and Ibn Qudama, al-Mughni; it was not reviewed by a scholar.

## Added in v1.2 (Extension 5)

- **Fiqh sources:** the answer key and the retrieval corpus of Extension 5 refer to pages of the Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia (Shamela), islamweb.net, binbaz.org.sa, binothaimeen.net and alifta.gov.sa. Their texts are not redistributed; `fiqh_v12/corpus_manifest.json` lists URLs and checksums only.
- **Models:** Jais-3-MoVA-36B-A4B and Qwen3-30B-A3B under their own model card licenses.
- **Review:** neither scholars nor native speakers of Arabic reviewed the questions, the answer key or the Arabic texts.
