# Changelog

## v1.2 (2026-11-01)

- **Added Extension 5:** disagreement between the four Sunni schools, Ibn Bāz and Ibn ʿUthaymīn on ten disputed fiqh questions; a context test with four conditions (no context, oracle passages, BM25 top 3, wrong passages); seven Usul reading cases. Models: Jais-3-MoVA-36B-A4B and Qwen3-30B-A3B. Everything is in `fiqh_v12/`.
- **Report:** new section Extension 5, new section *Review status and invitation*, abstract and key findings extended (EN, AR), corrections 11 to 14, reproducibility and sources extended.
- **Data policy unchanged:** no raw hadith text, no unmodified Quran text, no third-party passages. Results of Extension 5 are published without raw output and thinking text; checksums of the full originals are in `fiqh_v12/results/FULL_FILES.sha256`.
- Extensions 1 to 4, task files and checksums of v1.0 are unchanged.
